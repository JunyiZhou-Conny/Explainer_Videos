Laplace Mechanism Playground

Two neighbouring hospital databases differ only in Alice's row. In world x the true count is 41; in world x′ it is 41 + S. The curator adds noise of scale S/ε. Pick ε, then play the attacker and try to tell the worlds apart.

The guarantee is eε/(1 + eε) for a fifty-fifty prior, whatever the attacker knows. Each extra answer spends another ε, and the evidence needle can move by at most ε per answer.

Things to try

  1. Set ε = 0.1 and play ten rounds with one answer each. Can you beat 52.5%?
  2. Switch to Gaussian. Where does the log-ratio leave the ±ε band? Why does that break the definition?
  3. Switch to Uniform. Find an output that is possible in one world and impossible in the other.
  4. Keep ε = 0.5 and ask the same question ten times before guessing. What is your total budget, and how sure are you now?
  5. Raise S to 2 (a histogram query). The worlds move apart, but the noise grows too. Does your accuracy change?
