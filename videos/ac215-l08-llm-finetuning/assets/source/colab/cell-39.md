**Why 375.8 million and not 600 million?** Qwen3-0.6B has 596 million parameters. `bitsandbytes` stores two 4-bit weights in one 8-bit element, so `numel()` counts the 440.4 million weights of the quantized linear layers as 220.2 million. The 155.6 million embedding weights (and the small normalization layers) are not quantized and are counted in full: 155.6 + 220.2 = 375.8 million.
