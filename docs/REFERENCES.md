original preset descriptions, portable packaging and Swiss scene implementation. The existing
dark mathematical toolkit predates this incubator update and retains its Git attribution.

different kinds of provenance; do not infer one from another.

Review covered timestamped frames across the runtimes, with denser frame inspection and
on-screen text extraction in selected passages. It did not establish continuous soundtrack
listening. Each preset distinguishes visible observations from newly chosen colors, sizes,
timings and teaching recipes. These are interpretations, not recovered creator presets.

The making-of reference supports a workflow of written briefs, explicit design/engineering
handoffs, samples, review and iteration. Its named models and services are choices in that
production, not requirements of this repository. A useful portable skill records successful
decisions and repeatable steps, then proves them on another topic.

## Assets and rights

Fonts and speech assets are fetched from the URLs in [the manifest](../assets/manifest.json)
and checked against expected hashes. Inter and Noto font license files are downloaded beside
the fonts; the model's license is downloaded with its files. Those large assets are ignored by
Git. Third-party papers already in `library/` retain their original rights. The repository had
no declared blanket license before this update, and none is added here.
