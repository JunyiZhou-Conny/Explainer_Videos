## What is and is not established

- The seven release masters passed complete FFmpeg decoding and have H.264 video/AAC audio metadata recorded in [release-media.json](../catalog/release-media.json). This publication does not establish new human listening or factual review for the older episodes.
- The Swiss video has separate [review notes](../videos/skills-ecosystem/REVIEW.md), [source notes](../videos/skills-ecosystem/SOURCES.md), and [caption sidecars](../videos/skills-ecosystem/delivery). Its narration mentions the seven presets available at recording time; the incubator also catalogs the older `3b1b` family.
- All three formerly separate production lines were merged into `main` by [PR #5](https://github.com/JunyiZhou-Conny/Explainer_Videos/pull/5) (2026-10-13): the post-PR1 toolkit line (`52c3234`, kept at tag-like branch `archive/post-pr1-52c3234`), the CareOneX series ([PR #2](https://github.com/JunyiZhou-Conny/Explainer_Videos/pull/2)), and the AC215 L1 pilot ([PR #3](https://github.com/JunyiZhou-Conny/Explainer_Videos/pull/3)). Episode sources now live on `main`.
- The media archive release re-hosts every formerly branch-pinned variant byte-identically (`tools/rehost_media.py` pins each file's sha256); Mandarin-narration files carry a `.zh` suffix in their asset names.
- Existing main history, including the older pre-score Mandarin Tic-Tac-Toe cut, remains intact in git history. The catalog selects the newer scored variant.
- The AC215 L1 metadata (29:52, 1080p60) is the producer's record from [PR #3](https://github.com/JunyiZhou-Conny/Explainer_Videos/pull/3); this publication adds no new listening review.
- The untracked opening excerpt, scratch renders, third-party style references and unrelated local demos are not included in this finished-video collection.
