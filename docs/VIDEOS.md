## What is and is not established

- The seven release masters passed complete FFmpeg decoding and have H.264 video/AAC audio metadata recorded in [release-media.json](../catalog/release-media.json). This publication does not establish new human listening or factual review for the older episodes.
- The Swiss video has separate [review notes](../videos/skills-ecosystem/REVIEW.md), [source notes](../videos/skills-ecosystem/SOURCES.md), and [caption sidecars](../videos/skills-ecosystem/delivery). Its narration mentions the seven presets available at recording time; the incubator also catalogs the older `3b1b` family.
- The main branch at inspection contained earlier math renders. The post-PR1 branch `52c3234` contains complete scene sources for the making-of and short adaptation as well as newer caption/language variants. Those updates are linked, not merged wholesale.
- CareOneX source and six variants are already public in [PR #2](https://github.com/JunyiZhou-Conny/Explainer_Videos/pull/2), pinned here at `a70e4e2`. The release E2 is the newer branch artifact, not the older Downloads copy. The PR remains independent.
- [MLOps / AC215 Lecture 1 in PR #3](https://github.com/JunyiZhou-Conny/Explainer_Videos/pull/3) has script/helpers but no rendered pilot in the inspected tree. It is not listed as a completed video.
- Existing main history, including the older Mandarin Tic-Tac-Toe cut, remains intact. The catalog selects the newer branch variant.
- The untracked opening excerpt, scratch renders, third-party style references and unrelated local demos are not included in this finished-video collection.
